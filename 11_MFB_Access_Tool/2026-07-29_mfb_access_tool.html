Week 31 Topic 6 of 12: Bed Selection Standalone HTML · No external dependencies

Bed assignment is often done from memory or informal hallway conversation — leading to mismatches: a bariatric patient in a standard-weight-rated room, a high-elopement-risk patient in a distant hallway, or a ventilator-dependent admit in a room without vent infrastructure.

This tool standardizes the decision in under 60 seconds: enter 7–9 clinical parameters, get a prioritized bed type recommendation with rationale, conflict alerts, a pre-placement checklist, and a ready-to-copy Epic SmartPhrase.

Bed Type Priority Hierarchy

  1. Vent-Capable Room — highest clinical priority
  2. Bariatric Room — weight ≥ 350 lbs or bariatric lift specified
  3. Airborne Isolation Room — negative pressure required
  4. Private Isolation Room — contact/droplet/protective precautions
  5. Behavioral/Safety Room — elopement, aggression, or self-harm flags
  6. High-Acuity / Step-Down Capable Bed — IV meds, telemetry, frequent monitoring
  7. Ceiling Lift-Equipped Room — when not already captured by bariatric
  8. Standard Rehab Bed — default when no flags present

Epic SmartPhrase Pairing

Suggested SmartPhrase name: .MFBBEDREC

BED SELECTION — REHAB ADMISSION Program: [Rehab Program] Recommended Bed Type: [Type] Additional Requirements: [if any] Medical Acuity: [low/moderate/high] Isolation: [type or none] Behavioral/Safety Flags: [list or None] Equipment Needs: [list or Standard] CONFLICTS: [if any] Rationale: [auto-generated clinical rationale] Pre-placement checklist completed per MFB Access protocol. Charge RN notified. Bed board updated. Clinical judgment governs final placement.

Testing Checklist

  • Bariatric trigger: weight 350 → "Bariatric Room"; weight 349 → Standard
  • Vent flag: check "Ventilator dependent" → "Vent-Capable Room"
  • Priority: Vent + Bariatric → Vent-Capable Room primary, Bariatric as additional
  • Conflict: Vent + Bariatric → red conflict alert box appears
  • Isolation: Airborne → "Airborne Isolation Room (Negative Pressure)"
  • Behavioral: elopement OR aggression OR suicide → "Behavioral / Safety Room"
  • Copy button: copies SmartPhrase to clipboard
  • Reset: clears all fields and hides results
  • Print view: results visible, buttons hidden
  • Mobile: single column at <520px width
  • PHI banner: sticky at top at all times
  • No localStorage: no PHI stored anywhere
